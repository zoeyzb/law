Practice areas

  • Grand jury subpoenas, indictments and federal trials. We engage early, often before charges, to shape how the government sees your case.

  • We challenge the stop, the field tests and the breath or blood evidence, and fight to protect your license and your record.

  • Fraud, embezzlement and securities investigations, handled with forensic accountants and a quiet, strategic approach.

  • Possession through trafficking. We scrutinize every search, warrant and informant behind the evidence.

  • Serious allegations demand a disciplined defense: self-defense, disputed identity and unreliable accounts.

  • We read the whole record for legal error and argue for reversal, a new trial or a reduced sentence.
